About Me

Education

September 1979 – July 1983

Undergraduate studies, majoring in Automation, at Shenyang Institute of Technology, Shenyang, China.

September 1986 – July 1989

Postgraduate studies, majoring in Control Theory, at Beijing Institute of Technology, Beijing, China.

September 1989 – May 1993

Doctoral studies, majoring in Control Theory, at Tsinghua University, Beijing, China.

Date & place of birth: November 25, 1962; Tangshan, Hebei Province, China.

Weicun Zhang retired in November 2022 from the School of Automation and Electrical Engineering at the University of Science and Technology Beijing, where he pursued academic work in control theory and engineering.

Visiting Appointments

Visiting Research Fellow, Department of Industrial and Operations Engineering, University of Michigan at Ann Arbor (February 1997 – May 1998).

Visiting Professor, Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering, Seoul National University (September 2006 – August 2007).

His earlier academic work focused on adaptive control, multiple-model adaptive control, and nonlinear systems. His current research interests include analytic number theory, particularly the Riemann Hypothesis, the Extended Riemann Hypothesis (for Dedekind zeta functions), the Generalized Riemann Hypothesis (for Dirichlet L-functions), and the Grand Riemann Hypothesis (for cuspidal automorphic L-functions).
