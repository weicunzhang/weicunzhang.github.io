About Me

Weicun Zhang retired in November 2022 from the School of Automation and Electrical Engineering at the University of Science and Technology Beijing, where he pursued academic work in control theory and engineering.

His earlier academic work focused on adaptive control, multiple-model adaptive control, and nonlinear systems. His current research interests include analytic number theory, particularly the Riemann zeta function, Dirichlet L-functions, and related questions concerning zeros of zeta and L-functions.

Research Interests
